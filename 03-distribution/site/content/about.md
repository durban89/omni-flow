CondomIQ is an independent publication about condoms and sexual health. We explain fit, materials, lubrication and correct use in plain language, for readers in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Hong Kong and Taiwan.

We do not sell condoms. We do not run a shop, take orders or hold stock. Our only product is the guide you are reading.

## What we cover

- **Fit and sizing** — how to measure, how size charts differ between brands, and what a poor fit actually costs you.
- **Materials** — latex, polyurethane, polyisoprene and natural membrane, and who should avoid which.
- **Correct use** — the failure modes that show up again and again in real-world data, and how to avoid them.
- **Troubleshooting** — breakage, slippage, irritation and loss of sensation, described without euphemism.

## How a guide is written

1. **Sourcing first.** Each article starts from public-health guidance and peer-reviewed literature — the CDC, WHO, NHS, FDA, and journals indexed in PubMed — not from product marketing.
2. **Drafting.** We use AI-assisted drafting to turn those sources into readable prose, then edit for accuracy, tone and clarity.
3. **Checking.** Every article lists the sources it relies on at the bottom of the page. If a claim cannot be traced to a named source, it does not belong in the article.
4. **Updating.** Guidance changes. When a cited source is revised, we revise the article and update the date shown on the page.

We are not a clinic and we do not provide individual medical advice. If you have symptoms, an allergic reaction, a possible exposure or a question about your own health, please see a clinician — see our [Medical & Affiliate Disclaimer](/disclaimer/).

## How we make money

Some outbound product links are affiliate links. If you buy through one, the retailer may pay us a commission at no extra cost to you. These links are marked on the page, and they never determine what we recommend or how we rank options. You can read the full terms in our [Medical & Affiliate Disclaimer](/disclaimer/).

## Corrections

We would rather be corrected than be wrong. If you find an error, an outdated citation or a claim that overstates the evidence, email us with the page URL and the source you are relying on. We review every report and publish corrections on the article itself.
