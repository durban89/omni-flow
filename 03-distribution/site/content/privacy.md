CondomIQ is a static website. We do not have user accounts, a newsletter, comments or a contact form, and we do not ask you to sign in. This policy explains the small amount of data that is still processed when you visit the site, and what you can do about it.

## 1. Who is responsible

The data controller for this website is CondomIQ ("we", "us"). You can reach us at {{< email privacy >}}.

## 2. What we process

**Server and security logs.** Like every website, our hosting infrastructure processes technical request data in order to serve pages and block abuse: your IP address, browser user-agent string, the URL requested, the referring page, and the time of the request. This data is processed by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, Inc., on our behalf. We use it only for delivering the site, security and aggregate traffic volume. We do not use it to build profiles of individual readers, and we do not combine it with any other data.

**No cookies or tracking of our own.** The site sets no cookies. We do not run Google Analytics, advertising pixels, session recording, fingerprinting or cross-site tracking of any kind. If we ever add privacy-friendly analytics, we will update this policy first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent.

**Affiliate redirects.** Product links on the site point to `/out/…` on our own domain, which forwards you to the retailer. The redirect sets no cookies and is marked `noindex`. The redirect request is processed by the same infrastructure as any other page request, so it appears in the logs described above. We do not record which reader clicked which link.

**Email.** If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We keep it only as long as needed to answer you and to handle any follow-up, then delete it.

## 3. What we do not collect

We do not knowingly collect: names, postal addresses, phone numbers, payment details, health information, precise location, or any special-category data. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Why we are allowed to process it

Under the EU and UK GDPR our legal bases are:

- **Legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f)) — operating a secure website, preventing abuse and understanding aggregate traffic.
- **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)) — only where we would ask for it, for example if we introduced optional analytics.
- **Legal obligation** (Art. 6(1)(c)) — where we must retain something to comply with the law.

## 5. Who else is involved

- **Cloudflare, Inc.** — hosting, content delivery, DNS and the affiliate redirect service. Cloudflare processes request data on our behalf under its data processing terms and may transfer data internationally using Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard.
- **Retailers and affiliate networks** — once you follow an outbound product link you are on someone else's site, and their privacy policy applies. They may set their own cookies to attribute the referral. We do not control that and receive only aggregate commission reports, not information about individual readers.

We do not otherwise share data, except where required by law or to protect the site from attack.

## 6. How long we keep it

Edge and security logs are retained by Cloudflare for a short, limited period under its own retention rules. We do not maintain a separate copy. Emails are deleted once the matter is resolved.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- know whether we hold personal data about you, and receive a copy;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- have data erased;
- restrict or object to processing;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on it;
- lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — for example the ICO in the UK, or your national data protection authority in the EU.

California residents have the right to know, delete, correct and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and to use an authorised agent. **We do not sell or share personal information** as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not knowingly collect information from consumers under 18.

Because we hold almost no identifiable data, a request may result in us confirming that we hold nothing that can be linked to you. To make a request, email {{< email privacy >}}. We respond within the timeframe required by applicable law, normally within 30 days, and we will not charge you or treat you differently for exercising your rights.

## 8. Children

This site is written for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a minor has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to our accounts is protected by strong authentication. No system is perfectly secure, but the small surface area of a static site — no database, no login, no forms — substantially limits what could be exposed.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles data, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will be noted on the home page for a reasonable period.
